Sotto 0.2.7: inviting people that just works.

- **Copy buttons work.** Copying your tag, invite links and safety numbers failed silently in the app. Fixed everywhere.
- **Claim a tag in seconds.** Sotto suggests one from your name, and claiming takes a few seconds. If the relay can't be reached, you're told so within half a minute instead of waiting.
- **Invite someone, simply.** Your full tag is front and centre with Copy tag and Copy invite message, which includes where to get Sotto.
- **Add someone by pasting.** Paste their tag, an invite link, or the whole invite message they sent; Sotto finds the tag or link in it.
- **See your connection.** The sidebar says "Connected directly" as soon as you unlock, or names the relay it can't reach.

On a copy older than 0.2.6, install this version by hand once from vorynza.cloud. Updates after that finish on their own.

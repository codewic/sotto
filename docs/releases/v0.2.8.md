Sotto 0.2.8: group calls that reach everyone.

- **Group calls ring the whole group.** Every member rings, whether or not you have a conversation with them. Missed it? Anyone in the call can ring the group again.
- **No more lost group messages after a relay restart.** A relay restarted twice in a row could reuse message numbers, and members silently dropped those messages. Numbers now only ever grow. Update your relay to get this.
- **Get the file you were sent.** After safe review, text files (scripts, code, notes) save as text under their own name, exactly as written, and any file can be saved as the original after a clear warning.
- **Group avatars show who's in the group,** built from members' own avatars.
- **Resize the sidebar and the conversation list** by dragging their edges. The sidebar snaps to icons when narrow.
- **The call window matches the main window,** and Back to Sotto tucks a call away while it keeps going.
- **Message tools stay put** while you move the pointer to them.

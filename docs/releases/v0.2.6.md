Sotto 0.2.6: updates that finish on any connection.

- **Updates no longer get cut off.** Earlier versions gave an update 40 seconds to download, which wasn't enough on slower connections. Now there's no time limit: a download that drops carries on from where it stopped, and Settings shows how far along it is.
- **Quieter scrollbars.** A slim thumb with no arrow buttons, in every pane.

If your copy shows "The update download was cut off", install this version by hand once from the releases page or vorynza.cloud. Updates after it finish on their own.

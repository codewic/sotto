Sotto 0.2.2: photos, voice notes and video, opened safely.

- **Photos.** Send images as photos and Sotto cleans them first: turned upright, location, camera details and times removed, very large ones resized. Choose Original file to send the exact bytes. Photos show in the conversation at their real shape, several sent together become an album, and a full-screen viewer zooms, pans, steps through them and saves a copy.
- **Voice notes.** Press the microphone when the message box is empty, record, listen back, then send. Each plays with a waveform you can click to jump around, at 1x, 1.5x or 2x. In High risk mode Sotto mentions once that voices can identify people; in Extreme it asks every time.
- **Video.** Send videos with their location and camera details removed, or as the original file. They show their first frame and length, and play full screen.
- **Nothing you receive is opened directly.** Every photo, voice note and video from someone else is decoded only inside the sealed sandbox Safe review uses (no network, no access to your files), and what you see or hear is a fresh copy rebuilt from its pixels or sound. In Extreme mode, and for the anonymous inbox, media waits until you choose Show.
- **Tidier storage.** Clean copies, and Safe review results, are removed once the message they came from is deleted or disappears.
- **Calls are set up for you.** Sotto now comes with its own call server, so calls work without any setup. Organizations can still use their own in Settings.
- **Needs ffmpeg.** The .deb installs it. With the AppImage, install it yourself (sudo apt install ffmpeg) to play voice notes and video.

Sotto 0.2.9: a mark of its own.

- **New Sotto mark.** A message bubble with a padlock shackle and three quiet dots, drawn on its own instead of on a coloured square, so Sotto no longer looks like the other violet apps beside it.
- **New app icon** in your launcher and dock: the mark on a white plate.
- **The loading mark draws the new outline,** and the shackle clicks down when you hover the mark.

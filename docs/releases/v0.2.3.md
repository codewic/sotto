Sotto 0.2.3: connects out of the box, in its own window.

- **Connects without setup.** Earlier versions pointed at a relay that only existed on a developer's computer, so a fresh install couldn't connect or claim a tag. Sotto now comes with its own relay at relay.cmigl.org, reached over an encrypted connection checked against certificates built into the app. An install set up with the old address moves to the new relay the next time you unlock.
- **Directly or through Tor, your choice.** New installs connect directly (Standard protection): the relay sees your network address, never your messages or who you talk to. Choose Through Tor in Settings, Connection if the relay shouldn't learn where you are either. If Tor is chosen but not running, the sidebar now says so and takes you to the fix.
- **Tags across relays** work with public relays as well as Tor ones.
- **Sotto's own window.** The system title bar is gone: a slim bar carries the Sotto mark and the minimize, maximize and close buttons, on every screen.
- **For organizations:** run your own relay from the same signed release with the Coolify package in the source repository, and enter its address in Settings.
